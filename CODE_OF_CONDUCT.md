# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in OpenContinuity respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's memory export, credentials, logs, or personal data.
- Use fictional data in reproductions unless you have explicit permission to publish the material.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, credential sharing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or disruptive. For a sensitive conduct or security concern, use a private contact channel published by the repository owner rather than opening a public issue. Until such a channel is listed, do not include sensitive details in any report.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
